# Candidates — The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse

Judge: house judge model, pairwise, no ties. Criteria in the judge's own
order:
1. on-subject — one line that is an excuse for a sick day
2. funny — funny on first read, through a turn, an understatement, or a gap between the excuse and the illness
3. original — a reason nobody else would send, not the standard cough-and-fever
4. voice — sounds like a real message to a real boss, not a joke told about one
5. tight — lands without a setup it does not need

Scored /25 (5 per criterion). Gate: one line, UTF-8, 1–200 chars, not only
whitespace. A line that explains its own joke is weaker; gross-out for its own
sake does not count.

## 1. goldfish bereavement — 24/25 — PICKED
I can't come in — my goldfish died and I need to grieve, and HR's policy on bereavement doesn't specify species.
(113 chars. On-subject 5, funny 5, original 5, voice 4, tight 5. The excuse
starts ordinary ("my goldfish died") and the punch is pure bureaucracy: the
policy doesn't specify species. The gap between a dead goldfish and an HR
loophole is the whole joke. Loses a point on voice: "I can't come in" is a
slightly flat opening.)

## 2. fluent in an unknown language — 22/25
I woke up fluent in a language nobody speaks, and until I figure out which one, I'm a liability in meetings.
(111 chars. On-subject 5, funny 4, original 5, voice 4, tight 4. A genuinely
strange excuse with a practical kicker ("a liability in meetings"). Loses on
funny: the turn is clever rather than laugh-out-loud, and on tight: the second
clause is doing two jobs.)

## 3. severe case of Monday — 22/25
The doctor says I have a severe case of Monday. Bed rest until further notice, possibly forever.
(99 chars. On-subject 5, funny 4, original 3, voice 5, tight 5. Clean and
quotable, and "possibly forever" is a good close. Loses on original: "a case
of the Mondays" is a well-worn joke, and the judge has seen it.)

## 4. the bed filed a complaint — 22/25
My bed has filed a formal complaint about my attendance record, and I have to stay home to negotiate.
(103 chars. On-subject 5, funny 4, original 4, voice 4, tight 5. Personifying
the bed as HR is a neat inversion. Loses on funny: the punch is gentle, and
"negotiate" lands softer than the goldfish's policy loophole.)

## 5. the sneeze that saw the future — 21/25
I sneezed so hard I saw the future, and in it I was still in bed. I'm just following medical advice from tomorrow.
(112 chars. On-subject 4, funny 4, original 5, voice 4, tight 4. The time-travel
sneeze is the most original image in the set. Docked on-subject — it is more a
vision than an illness — and on tight at 112 chars across two sentences.)

## 6. Wi-Fi caught what I have — 21/25
I can't come in today — my Wi-Fi caught what I have, and now neither of us can get out of bed.
(96 chars. On-subject 4, funny 4, original 4, voice 5, tight 4. Short and
relatable in the work-from-home era. Docked on-subject: it excuses the
internet, not the worker, and the illness itself is never named.)

## 7. cat submitted my resignation — 20/25
My cat sat on my laptop and submitted my resignation by accident; I'm staying home to un-resign before anyone notices.
(119 chars. On-subject 3, funny 4, original 4, voice 4, tight 5. A good
premise and a funny verb ("un-resign"). Docked on-subject: it is an excuse for
staying home to fix a resignation, not a sick-day excuse.)

## 8. allergy to my commute — 20/25
I've come down with a sudden, violent allergy to my commute. Symptoms flare up the moment I look at my shoes.
(112 chars. On-subject 5, funny 3, original 3, voice 5, tight 4. Believable
enough to almost send. Loses on funny and original: "allergic to work" is a
familiar gag, and the shoe symptom is the only fresh beat.)

## Revision of the winner

Draft: "I can't come in — my goldfish died and I need to grieve, and HR's policy on bereavement doesn't specify species." (113 chars)
Revised: "My goldfish died and I need to grieve; HR's bereavement policy doesn't specify species." (87 chars)

The "I can't come in" opening came out — the excuse is implied by the
message itself — and a semicolon took the place of the second "and", so the
grief and the loophole sit side by side as a deadpan claim instead of a
story. Twenty-six characters shorter (helps the size tie-break: exact ties go
to the smaller text). `main.py` was written then edited (thinking_revised: 2
writes in this session).
